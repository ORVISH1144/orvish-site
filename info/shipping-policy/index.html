Shipping Policy

At ORVISH, we aim to deliver your order quickly and safely across India. We partner with trusted courier companies to ensure fast and secure delivery of every order.

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1 working day after they are placed. Once your order is processed, it is handed over to our courier partner for shipment. We understand that every order can be a special purchase for yourself or a gift for someone you care about, and we take extra care to ensure safe packaging and delivery.

You will receive an email when your order is confirmed and when it is shipped, with the courier tracking details. You can also follow your order any time from Track order.

Delivery Time & Shipping Charges

  • Estimated delivery: 3 – 6 working days across India. Remote areas and the North Eastern Region may take a little longer.
  • Shipping charges: ₹49 per order, and free delivery on orders above ₹999.

Delivery timelines may vary depending on your location, courier operations, or public holidays.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Where Cash on Delivery is available, you will see it as a payment option at checkout. Online payments are accepted by UPI, cards and net banking through our secure payment partner.

Undelivered or Refused Parcels

Shipping charges are non-refundable for packages that are undelivered, unclaimed, or refused by the customer. Please make sure your address and phone number are correct at checkout.
