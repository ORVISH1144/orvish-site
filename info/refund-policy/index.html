Return & Refund Policy

Return Policy

At ORVISH, we strive to ensure that every customer has a great experience with our products. However, due to the nature of fragrance products, certain return conditions apply.

Non-Returnable Products

Perfumes and fragrance oils are non-returnable products due to hygiene and safety reasons. We do not offer refunds or exchanges for fragrances that have been opened or used.

Damaged or Defective Products

Returns or replacements will only be accepted if the product received is damaged or defective. Customers must report such issues within 5 days of receiving the parcel.

One-Time Replacement Policy

We offer a one-time replacement if you are not satisfied with the fragrance. Conditions:

  • Replacement is only available for orders ₹500 or above.
  • Orders with value below ₹500 are not eligible for replacement.
  • Replacement is allowed only once per order.

Shipping Charges

  • Shipping charges are non-refundable.
  • Any cost incurred by the customer to return the product will not be reimbursed.
  • The customer will be responsible for the return shipping cost.
  • Shipping costs are also non-refundable in cases where packages are undelivered, unclaimed, or refused by the customer.
  • The only exception applies if the error occurred from our side during shipping.

Return Instructions

If you need to return a product, please follow the instructions below:

  • Returns must be requested within 5 days of receiving the order. You can raise the request from My account → Orders, or contact us on WhatsApp.
  • Carefully pack the product securely to avoid damage during transit.
  • Write the following details clearly on the return parcel: Order ID, Full Name and Email Address.

If the returned product is received damaged due to improper packaging, the product may be discarded upon arrival and no refund or replacement will be issued.

Important Notes

Returns will not be accepted in cases of:

  • Damage caused by improper use or handling
  • Damage due to customer negligence
  • Products returned without proper packaging

Refund Policy

At ORVISH, refunds are processed in accordance with our store policies and are subject to approval by our customer support team. If you wish to request a refund, please contact our customer support team to verify whether your order is eligible for a return or refund.

Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment source used during checkout. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, customers will be required to provide their bank account details so that the refund can be processed.

Processing Time

Once a refund request is approved, the refund will be processed within a reasonable time frame. Refunds may take up to 7 working days to reflect in your account depending on the payment provider or bank processing time.

Payment Gateway Charges

All refunds are subject to a 2.5% deduction, which covers payment gateway processing fees.

Failed Online Transactions

If an online payment fails but the amount is deducted from your bank account, the refund is automatically processed back to the original payment source. Please allow up to 7 working days for the amount to reflect in your account.

Note: ORVISH does not hold this amount. The refund is handled by the payment gateway or banking partner.
