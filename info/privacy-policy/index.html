Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ORVISH (orvish.com) collects, uses and protects your information.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address (including a map location if you choose one).
  • Account details: if you sign in with email, Google or phone, we keep your sign-in details so you can see your orders and reuse your saved address.
  • Reviews: the name, rating and comment you submit.
  • Website activity: which pages are viewed, products added to cart, the device type, and an approximate location (city or area) worked out from your internet connection. This is used only to understand how the site is used. Your device is recognised by a random ID, not by your name.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, and to send you important order updates by email.
  • To handle returns, refunds and customer support.
  • To improve our products, pricing and website.

Payments

Online payments are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay). Your card, UPI or bank details are entered on their secure page. We never see or store your card details.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with services needed to run the store: our payment partner, courier companies (name, phone and address for delivery), and our hosting, sign-in and email providers.

Cookies and local storage

We store small pieces of data on your device to keep your cart, remember your saved address and keep you signed in.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your personal information at any time by writing to support@orvish.com or on WhatsApp at +91 81569 53937.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
